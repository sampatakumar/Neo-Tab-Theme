# Privacy Policy for NeoMorph Theme Extension

**Effective Date**: August 1, 2026

## 1. Overview
NeoMorph Theme ("the Extension") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our commitment to protecting user privacy while using the Extension.

## 2. Information Collection and Use
- **No Data Collection**: The Extension does **NOT** collect, store, track, or transmit any personally identifiable information, browsing history, location data, or web activity.
- **Local Storage**: All user settings, including custom uploaded background images/videos, bookmark shortcuts, theme selections, and To-Do list items, are stored locally on your device using `chrome.storage.local`.
- **No Third-Party Sharing**: We do not sell, trade, or transfer any user data to third parties.
